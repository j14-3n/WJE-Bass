About Me

A not-so-long, but a meaningful journey.

I began the double bass at the age of 13 (in 2019). It started in my high school wind band, where I met my first teacher - Eugenia Lou. She inspired me to learn the bass at a deeper level, which led me to participate in different ensembles outside the wind orchestra.

I then have the chance to play in string orchestra (Permata Seni Muzik, also known as Genius Seni Muzik). This was where I have met one of the most influential conductors in my journey - Datuk Mustafa Fuzer Nawi. I have also had the chance to learn from Foo Yin Hong, and Lin Yi Rou through workshops and masterclasses. Then came my first full symphony orchestra experience with the Selangor Symphony Youth Orchestra, I was invited to play as the principal bassist for the Malaysia tour in 2023. Then met a few friends with the same passion for music, where we formed small ensemble groups for competitions (Di Attacca String Ensemble, and the Melomane String Quintet). We have received awards in competitions such as the Singapore Raffles International Music Competition and the Euroaisa Music Competition.

Later on, I had the opportunity to join the Malaysian Philharmonic Youth Orchestra (in 2024), where I began to learn from my second, and current, teacher - Wolfgang Steike (Double Bass principal of Malaysian Philharmonic Orchestra). Mr Wolfgang and his wife, Ms Junhee Chae, have been great mentors to me, and have helped me to improve my technique and maturity in my musicality. They have taught me the importance of musicality, and how to express myself through music, both as a soloist and as an ensemble player.

Greater opportunities came my way, where I was selected to play with the Asian Youth Orchestra in 2026, National Symphony Orchestra, and RondoFest 2026.
I hope to continue to grow as a musician, and to inspire others through my music.

Check out my Instagram for more updates on my journey as a double bassist!

I have a few idols on the double bass (Rinat Ibragimov, Ödön Rácz and more), but Mikyung Sung is my favourite!
The audio playing as you read my site is my favourite double bass piece played by my favourite double bass player!
